## 1. What Cetele is

A mobile-first **group dhikr tracker**, shipped as an installable PWA. Dopamine mechanics (tap counter, progress
rings, streaks, live collective counter) layered on **real group accountability**, with "never miss twice"
forgiveness so a broken streak doesn't cause permanent quit. A _cetele_ is a circle that splits and completes a
shared dhikr goal together. Full spec: [`docs/PRD.md`](../docs/PRD.md).

Stack: Next.js App Router + React + TypeScript + Tailwind · Supabase (Postgres/Auth/Realtime) · Vercel · PWA, no app store.

**Measured against the 0023 predicate: the rebuilt chain does not merely shorten, `refresh_streak`
finds no kept day to anchor on and writes nothing at all** (STATUS said 10 → 1; the honest number
depends on the fixture, and 0 is what a rebuild produces). New `task_config_versions`, an interval
table on the 0023 shape — never deleted, an edit closes one row and opens the next, backfilled at
`tasks.created_at` so the config a task has now is the config it has always had and **nothing moves
until an admin actually edits something**. Maintained by a **trigger, not an RPC**, because 0007
grants `update (target_count)` on `tasks` directly to `authenticated`: there is no chokepoint to put
it in, and the direct PostgREST path would have stayed unversioned. **Target and cycle are versioned
together** — versioning the target alone leaves a second unversioned value inside the very expression
this exists to make as-of. `daily_completion` is fixed for free and it was the worse half: the nightly
rollup recomputes `[today-15, today-1]`, so a raise silently rewrote the last fortnight of consistency
downward while every older row kept the number it was written with — **two incompatible measurements
in one 90-day band with nothing marking the seam**. Idempotence is the property 0009's header claimed
and did not have; suite 013 now pins it.

**Then the e2e found something bigger, and it is the reason this entry is not just about targets.**
The new spec failed on a day-strip tick, and the mechanism was not the mirror: **every
timestamp→date reduction in the engine was done in UTC while `p_day` is the member's LOCAL date
(D34).** Those are different calendars for a large slice of every day. Measured on this stack with a
member in `Australia/Sydney`: at 14:29 UTC it is already 00:29 the next day there, so an edit made
"now" wrote a date that was the member's **yesterday**, and yesterday was re-judged at the new
target — the exact rewrite this migration exists to prevent, coming back in through the cast. It is
**one defect with three instances**, not three bugs: `tasks.created_at` (0021), `task_assignments`
(0023) and the new versions table. All three now go through **`private.user_date`**, and the client
mirrors follow. Frequency of occurrence is roughly `abs(offset)/24` of all edits — one in eight for
the +3 circles this app was built for. `lib/assignments.ts` had argued in a comment that reducing in
UTC was correct _because the SQL did the same_; the first half was true and the conclusion was wrong.
**Both were wrong together, which is exactly why a mirror agreeing with its original is not proof.**

**And that exposed a real bug in `useLocalToday` that had nothing to do with any of this.** It seeds
from the server's snapshot and only re-checks on a 30s interval or a wake-up event — so it never
re-checked when the TIMEZONE ITSELF changed. `TimezoneSync` (D44) writes the real zone on the first
render of a fresh account and refreshes, and the hook then held the **UTC-derived date for up to 30
seconds after the correction had landed** — measured: `timeZone: "Australia/Sydney"`,
`serverTodayISO: "2026-08-03"`, hook still returning `"2026-08-02"`. Everything downstream keys off
that value, so a member's taps went to the **wrong day** in that window — the silent mis-dating D44
exists to prevent — and once assignment and config resolve on the member's calendar, their whole task
list vanished, because a task assigned "today" is in the future to a client that thinks it is still
yesterday. One `check()` call at the top of the effect. **Pre-existing, invisible while dates were
UTC, and it is the symptom that led to all of the above.**

**Client mirrors:** new `lib/task-config.ts` (sibling of `lib/assignments.ts`) wired into the count
screen's day-strip, Today's `dayFull`/rings, the Progress grid and the admin breakdown — because the
engine being right is only half of it: the strip does its own comparison, and left on the live target
it would have un-ticked every day already kept while the streak went on counting them. Both mirrors
now keep **raw timestamps** and reduce per member, since the group breakdown scores several members
on several clocks and one pre-reduced date cannot serve two zones. `localDateISO` gained a
**formatter cache** — `toLocaleDateString` builds a fresh `Intl.DateTimeFormat` per call, and the
as-of predicates call it per task per day per member.

**Four more gaps found in the same audit and fixed, three of them pre-existing:**
**(1)** Progress's "N of 14 days" and the admin breakdown's score **ignored frequency entirely** —
they had been updated for assignment (0023) but not for cycles (0021), so both disagreed with
`is_day_complete` for any non-daily task. Both are SCORES and now mirror all three bounds.
**(2)** Today's `dayFull` filtered by the task set visible on the **selected** day, so which days the
strip marked complete depended on which day happened to be selected; it also folded in the member's
own denser cycle, judging them on occasions they had volunteered for — the precise failure D51 exists
to prevent. **(3)** Today's circle roster and collective counted **every member on the VIEWER's
day** — the §4 invariant the group hub was fixed for on 2026-07-25, on a screen that was left
behind; and its numerator was not scoped to who carries a task, so the same circle read two different
percentages on Today and on Group. **(4)** Manage's `task_assignments` query had **no group filter**
at all — RLS admits every circle the viewer belongs to, so an admin of five circles pulled all five
circles' rows into the payload of a screen about one. Nothing looked wrong because the client filtered
by task id downstream; the cost simply grew with how many circles the admin had joined.

### Branching — `main` is production, `staging` is the soak (D50, 2026-07-28)

**Two long-lived branches. Nothing reaches `main` that has not sat on `staging` first.**

| Branch    | Role                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `main`    | **Production.** Auto-deploys to `cetele-sable.vercel.app`. Only ever **fast-forwarded from `staging`** — never from a feature branch, never directly.    |
| `staging` | **The soak.** Integration target for every feature branch; deploys to the staging URL. Freely re-pushed; force-push allowed if it ever needs untangling. |

**The flow:**

1. Branch off **`staging`** (not `main`) — one per Linear issue, named to match Linear's (`mohidkhanzada/cet-N-slug`).
2. Commit increments; **push** for a Vercel preview URL.
3. **The four gates green** before merging to `staging`: `pnpm build` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit` · `pnpm format:check`. DB or core-loop work also needs `pnpm test:rls` + `pnpm test:e2e`.
4. Merge to **`staging`**, push, **delete the feature branch** (local + remote). This step does **not** need owner approval — staging is not production.
5. **Verify on the staging URL**, not just locally. This is the step the whole arrangement exists for.
6. **Ask the owner to promote.** On approval: fast-forward `main` from `staging`, push with the override, done. `main` is never merged into — if it can't fast-forward, something bypassed staging and that is the bug.

**The `main` gate is enforced locally by `.husky/pre-push`** — it refuses a push to `main` that is (a) not contained in
`origin/staging`, (b) not a fast-forward, or (c) missing the deliberate override:

```
ALLOW_MAIN_PUSH=1 git push origin main
```

Typing that override **is** the confirmation — it is meant to be impossible to do by reflex. Non-fast-forward and
branch-deletion on `main` have **no** override at all. Feature and staging pushes are untouched by the hook.

> **Local only.** A server-side GitHub ruleset would be the authoritative version, but the agent's GitHub account has
> **push, not admin**, on `mohid-yn/Cetele` (verified 2026-07-28) — so this cannot be set from here, and was
> deliberately deferred to the local hook alone. A push from another machine or the GitHub web UI is still unguarded.

Open a real PR only ad-hoc when a written review trail is wanted (`gh` is installed).

**Two things need explicit owner approval, separately:** promoting `staging` → `main` (= deploying), and
`supabase db push` (= migrating prod). Never assume one covers the other.

**Migrations** are the only sanctioned path for schema change — `supabase db push`, files named to match the applied
version (no drift). Every new table needs **explicit grants** (0006 set a default-privileges revoke, so a new table
starts at zero for _every_ role, `service_role` included) and **RLS from day one**, pinned by a pgTAP suite.

**Confirm consequential product decisions** with the user, then log them here in "Decisions that still bind."

**Keep the docs in sync:** edit [`docs/PRD.md`](../docs/PRD.md), then regenerate the Word copy with
`python3 scripts/build_prd_docx.py` (stdlib-only; the script _is_ the formatting — edit content there too).

**Track in Linear** (team `CET`, https://linear.app/mohidkz/team/CET) via the Linear MCP — issue state stays in sync
with the code.

**The user prefers concise, scannable docs** — tables and short bullets, not walls of text.

(2026-08-02 · 2026-08-03)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D55 | **The roadmap is a published programme a circle FOLLOWS, and its progress belongs to the MEMBER.** Content (`roadmaps`, `roadmap_items`, `roadmap_rewards`) is authored **by migration** — the badges-catalog pattern (0015) — not hardcoded in TS and not a CRUD screen. Hardcoding was rejected on **identity, not edit cost**: `roadmap_progress` needs a stable id to point at, and a TS constant lets a deploy rename or drop an item and silently orphan last year's records, which is exactly why the badge mark keys on `badges.id`. An authoring UI is **deferred, not refused** — because the content is already data, adding one later is purely additive. **A new year opens a new roadmap row; last year's is never edited or deleted** (D54's interval lesson; nothing a member earned is revoked). **A circle follows a roadmap by its OWNER's choice** (`groups.roadmap_id`, default null), never globally: the rewards are the administration's real-world promises ("travel covered", "the closing sitting"), and showing them to a circle it has never met makes the app promise on someone else's behalf. The nav tab appears only for a circle that follows one. **Progress is keyed `(member, item)`, never per group** — a member in two circles on the same programme has ONE record; the circle grants access, the member owns the progress. **Visible to the member, their circle's admins, and a super admin** (D31/D29's shape), and the member is TOLD so on the screen: progress recorded to claim a real reward is a claim, not a confidence. **The boundary that keeps D26/D27 intact:** the super-admin view is READ-ONLY and reads **roadmap tables only** — never `logs`, streaks, `daily_completion` or any circle's dhikr figures.
